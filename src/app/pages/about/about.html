Our Journey

Church History

Our spiritual journey of faith, prayer, worship, and God’s guidance in Berlin

January 2025

Beginning of the Ministry

El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church Berlin began through online worship gatherings, prayer meetings, and fellowship sessions. A small group of believers joined together with a vision to build a Christ-centered Malayalam Pentecostal fellowship in Berlin.

March 23, 2025

First Offline Worship

The church conducted its first in-person worship service at International Baptist Church Hall, Berlin Steglitz Rathaus. Families gathered together for worship, prayer, Bible meditation, and fellowship, marking a blessed milestone in the ministry journey.

Present Ministry

Growing in Faith

Today the church continues to grow spiritually through Sunday worship, fasting prayer, intercession meetings, youth fellowship, ladies meetings, Sunday school, and community gatherings — bringing people closer to God and strengthening faith among families in Berlin.
