Our Journey

Church History

Our spiritual journey of faith, prayer, worship, and God’s guidance in Berlin

January 2025

Beginning of the Ministry

The journey of El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church began with a God-given vision placed in the heart of Pr. Joshwa Sagaram while living in Bonn, Germany. Responding to God's calling, he moved to Berlin on January 1, 2025, with a desire to establish a Christ-centered ministry that would impact lives, strengthen believers, and bring people into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

First Sunday of January 2025

Online Fellowship Begins

On the first Sunday of January 2025, the church began gathering online through Zoom for prayer, worship, Bible meditation, and fellowship. Families and believers from different places joined together, laying the spiritual foundation for what God would continue to build in Berlin.

March 23, 2025

First Offline Worship

On March 23, 2025, El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church held its first in-person worship service in Berlin with 14 members. This marked a significant milestone as believers gathered physically to worship, pray, study God's Word, and experience Christian fellowship together.

Present Ministry

Growing in Faith

Today, the church continues to grow as a vibrant, Spirit-filled community. Through Sunday Worship, Intercessory Prayer Meetings, Sunday School, Ladies Fellowship, PYPA (Pentecostal Youth & Young Adults), Fasting Prayer, and special spiritual gatherings, lives are being strengthened and families are being encouraged through God's Word and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To be a Christ-centered church where people encounter God's presence, grow in faith, discover their purpose, and become disciples who impact their families, communities, and the world for Jesus Christ.

🙏

Our Mission

To worship God wholeheartedly, teach and live according to His Word, strengthen families, empower children and youth, pray for revival and transformation, and share the Gospel of Jesus Christ with people from every nation.
