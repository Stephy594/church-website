Greetings in the matchless name of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ!

Welcome to El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church

El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church is a vibrant, Spirit-filled community dedicated to worshiping God, growing in faith, and sharing the love of Jesus Christ with people from all nations.

Our journey began with a God-given vision given to Pr. Joshwa Sagaram while living in Bonn, Germany. In obedience to God's calling, he moved to Berlin on January 1, 2025, with a burden to establish a ministry that would impact lives, strengthen believers, and advance God's Kingdom.

What began as online prayer and worship gatherings through Zoom on the first Sunday of January 2025 soon grew into a thriving fellowship of believers united in prayer, worship, and Christian fellowship. As God continued to open doors, the church held its first in-person worship service on March 23, 2025 at Rathaus Steglitz, Berlin, with 14 members. Since then, the Lord has faithfully expanded the ministry and touched many lives through His Word and His Spirit.

Today, El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church serves as a spiritual home for individuals and families seeking a deeper relationship with God. We are passionate about creating an atmosphere where people can experience God's presence, discover their purpose, and grow into mature followers of Christ.

Our Mission
  • To worship God wholeheartedly and glorify His name.
  • To teach and live according to the truth of God's Word.
  • To build strong Christ-centered families and communities.
  • To empower children, youth, and future generations.
  • To pray for revival, healing, and transformation.
  • To share the Gospel of Jesus Christ with all people.
Join Us

We gather every Sunday for worship, prayer, Bible teaching, and fellowship. Throughout the month, we also conduct Intercessory Prayer Meetings, Ladies Fellowship, PYPA (Pentecostal Youth & Young Adults), Sunday School, and Fasting & Prayer Services.

Whether you are new to faith, searching for a church family, or desiring a deeper walk with God, you are always welcome here.

"A Place to Belong. A Place to Grow. A Place to Encounter God."
