About Our Church

El-Shaddai International Pentecostal Church Berlin is a Malayalam Pentecostal fellowship established to bring believers together in worship, prayer, fellowship, and spiritual growth in Germany.

The church began its journey in January 2025 through online prayer and worship meetings among Malayalam Christian families and believers living in Berlin and nearby areas.

As the fellowship continued to grow spiritually, God opened the opportunity to begin offline worship gatherings. In March 2025, regular in-person worship services officially started in Berlin, bringing together believers for praise, Bible teaching, prayer, and Christian fellowship.

Today, the church continues to serve the community through Sunday worship, fasting prayers, youth fellowship, ladies meetings, Sunday school, intercession prayer gatherings, and special spiritual meetings.

Our vision is to build a Christ-centered worship community where people can experience the love of God, grow in faith, and live spiritually strengthened lives through the guidance of the Holy Spirit.
